A few things to know.

Do guests need an account?

No. Your audience opens the event link or scans its QR code and chooses a language. If the organiser set a PIN, the event page will ask for it.

Can I choose my own microphone?

Yes. Use your computer’s microphone, an audio interface or a recorded audio file from the speaker console. Microphone capture requires HTTPS and browser permission.

Are the captions always accurate?

Captions and translations are automatic and can make mistakes. An organiser can fix recent captions and prepare vocabulary or a script before an event.

Is it free?

Hosting depends on your deployment’s free allowance and usage. Live speech and translation providers may charge separately. The interactive preview above uses sample text and makes no provider calls.
