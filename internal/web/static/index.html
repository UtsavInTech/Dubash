Good to know

Does the audience need an app or an account?

No. They scan the QR code or open the link and choose a language. If the organiser set a PIN, the page asks for it once.

Which microphone can the speaker use?

Any: the laptop’s own, a wireless mic through a mixer, or an audio interface. The console runs in the browser.

How accurate is it?

Very good on clear speech, and better with a dictation bank and script. Captions are automatic, so the speaker console lets you fix a word live; readers see the fix at once.

What does it cost?

Roughly ₹30 an hour for listening, plus about ₹4 an hour for each language people are reading.
