What Dubash does

Dubash helps event audiences follow live speech in their chosen language. With separate permission, an organiser may add a speaker profile, voice recordings and phrases to improve recognition and translation.

Your choices and permission

Each use is optional and separate. Recognition permission lets Dubash use recordings and the speaker’s word list to improve recognition. Storage permission sets a 30, 90 or 365 day deletion period. Consent can be withdrawn at any time; withdrawal stops the relevant use and deletes recordings.

An organiser may record permission only when they have the speaker’s written permission. Future synthetic speech requires the speaker’s own separate confirmation. Synthetic audio must be labelled to listeners. Dubash does not create synthetic voices in this phase.

Words and transcripts

Speaker phrases are stored as suggestions in the Dictation Bank. An organiser must approve a phrase before it can affect live captions. Event transcripts may be retained under the event’s own retention setting.

Access and deletion

Voice samples are private to authorised organisers. You may ask the organiser to correct your profile, withdraw permission or delete your recordings and profile information. Deletion can take a short time to propagate through backups.

Your rights

People in India may have rights under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, including access to information about processing, correction, erasure, grievance redressal and withdrawal of consent, subject to applicable law. Contact the organiser who collected the information to exercise these rights.
