Speaker privacy notice

Draft - to be reviewed by a lawyer. This draft needs legal review before public use.

What is collected

When a speaker profile is created, Dubash stores the speaker’s display name, spoken languages, style and organiser notes. If separately permitted, it stores voice recordings and optional transcripts, consent choices and versions, and phrases with their meanings and translations. Live events may also create audience transcripts.

Why and who can see it

Profile details, recordings and phrases are used to prepare recognition and translations for the speaker. Recordings and profiles are available only to authorised organisers using the protected studio. Audience members do not receive voice sample files. Suggested phrases are reviewed by organisers before use.

How long it is kept

Recordings are scheduled for automatic deletion after the chosen 30, 90 or 365 day period. The organiser can delete samples sooner. Withdrawal of recognition or storage permission removes samples. Event transcripts follow the retention and export settings of their event and may be deleted by the organiser.

Withdrawal and deletion

Contact the event organiser to withdraw permission or request access, correction or erasure. A withdrawal is recorded so the permission history remains auditable; it stops future use and deletes the recordings. Recognition withdrawal also retires the speaker’s suggested or approved Dictation Bank entries.

India DPDP Act 2023

Subject to applicable law, individuals may request information about personal data processing, correction and erasure, withdraw consent, raise a grievance and nominate another person to exercise rights. The organiser who collected the information is the contact for these requests.

Synthetic speech

Dubash does not create synthetic copies of a speaker’s voice in this phase. Any future feature needs separate confirmation from the speaker, clear labelling of synthetic speech, and deletion when permission is withdrawn.
