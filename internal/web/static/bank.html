What the speaker plans to say: it helps the correction stage tell a name from a mis-hearing. Rehearsal

Record a minute or two with the hard words (names, places, numbers) as a WAV file, and give the exact text that was said if you have it. You will see recognition without and with the bank, the corrections, the scores and the translations. Up to 3 minutes; it is billed twice.
